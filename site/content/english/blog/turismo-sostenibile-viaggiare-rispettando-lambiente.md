#### Sustainability ####
- Use renewable energy and energy-efficiency systems in accommodation facilities
- Adopt waste and water consumption management policies aimed at reducing waste
- Prefer local and zero-mile suppliers to reduce transport-related CO2 emissions

#### Supporting local communities
- Collaborate with local producers and artisans to offer travelers authentic experiences
- Invest in initiatives to safeguard local cultural and natural heritage
- Raise tourists' awareness of the importance of a respectful and conscious approach

#### Promoting sustainable mobility
- Encourage the use of low-impact means of transport, such as bicycles and electric vehicles
- Organize excursions and activities that promote discovering the area on foot or in "slow" mode
- Foster intermodality by combining different public and private transport solutions
Embracing sustainable tourism is not only an ethical imperative, but also a concrete opportunity for businesses to differentiate themselves, reduce costs and build loyalty among a clientele increasingly attentive to the impact of their travel choices. Investing in this direction means helping to preserve the planet's resources for future generations.
