Hospitality is a fundamental element in the non-hotel accommodation sector, where personalizing the service can make the difference between a mediocre review and a memorable experience. The first contact with the guest begins well before their arrival, through clear and proactive communication.

A crucial aspect is the creation of a professional welcome book, which should not be a simple instruction manual but a true guide to the stay experience. It should include not only practical information about the home, but also personalized suggestions about the neighborhood, recommended restaurants and local attractions.

The welcome kit is another distinctive element: small touches such as local products, a bottle of wine, or even just the essentials for breakfast can turn a simple check-in into a special moment. It is important to tailor these elements to the type of guest and the length of the stay.

Professional check-in management, whether in person or automated, must be flawless. This includes preparing the apartment, perfect cleaning, checking all systems and arranging any extras requested by the guest.
