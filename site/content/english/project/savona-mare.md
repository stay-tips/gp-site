# Holiday Home in Savona: A Profitable Investment Opportunity
The Ligurian Riviera is a unique territory, where scenic beauty intertwines with extraordinary real estate opportunities. In a geographical setting that combines sea, hills and historic villages, the province of Savona confirms itself as a dynamic and attractive hospitality market, capable of intercepting diversified tourist flows.
Our project arises from the idea of transforming a traditional property into a modern investment opportunity, one that knows how to interpret the new demands of experiential and conscious tourism. No longer just an apartment, but a hospitality experience that tells the story of the territory, enhances it and generates economic and cultural value.

![opportunity](images/project/savona-info-1.png)

## Benefits of the Service
The experience of enhancing the holiday home in the province of Savona tells the story of a smart investment that managed to transform a traditional property into a dynamic and modern income opportunity. Just 5 minutes from the sea, the apartment won over a varied tourist market, attracting families, groups of friends and travelers in search of an authentic Ligurian experience.

The numbers are clear: an annual yield of 6.8% with an occupancy rate of 78%, which translates into a 15% increase in property value and the amortization of renovation costs in just 4 years. No longer just a property, but a genuine profitable investment tool.

## Intervention Strategy
The real key to success was a well-thought-out renovation that managed to combine functionality and comfort. We completely rethought the spaces, creating a modular and contemporary environment that adapts to the different needs of guests.

![strategy](images/project/savona-info-2.png)

The apartment was designed to comfortably accommodate 5 people, with a distribution of spaces studied down to the smallest detail: a double bedroom for the couple, a twin bedroom for the children or friends, and a versatile triple bedroom. The main bathroom and the service bathroom, together with an open space kitchen and an equipped living area, complete the living offering.

The technologies installed made the difference: fast internet connection, air conditioning system, home automation and state-of-the-art appliances transformed the apartment into a smart and comfortable environment. A small balcony then offers that extra touch that allows you to fully enjoy the Ligurian dimension.

## Continuous Analysis and Improvements
Far from being a static project, the investment included a continuous and dynamic analysis of the market. Through constant monitoring of guest feedback, we implemented targeted improvements: from the adjustment of seasonal prices to the introduction of additional services.

The results were significant: a 40% increase in bookings, achieving Superhost status on the main rental platforms, and a diversification of the tourist market that made the apartment a point of reference for travelers from all over Europe.

The investment demonstrated how a careful strategy, attentive to detail and open to innovation, can transform a simple property into a dynamic and engaging income opportunity. Not just an apartment, but a project that reflects the ability to interpret the contemporary tourist market.
