## Benefits of the Service
Our project completely revolutionized the profitability and appeal of the apartment in the heart of Firenze. We transformed a traditional property into a hospitality gem capable of generating a stable and significant income. The benefits were immediate and measurable: a 5.5% increase in annual yield, with peaks of 7% during the high tourist season, and an occupancy rate that reached 85% on an annual basis.

The real innovation was the ability to attract different types of guests: from the cultural tourist to the smart worker, from the group of friends to the family in search of an authentic experience. The apartment became much more than a simple accommodation - a true living story of Firenze, able to offer total immersion in the city experience.

![method](images/project/florence-info-1.png)

## Intervention Strategy
The intervention followed a methodical and careful approach, focusing on three main areas: architecture, technology and design. We preserved the original historic elements - ceilings with exposed beams, restored terracotta floors - integrating them with state-of-the-art technologies.

The distribution of the spaces was completely rethought:
* Main double bedroom with panoramic view
* Two twin bedrooms with custom furnishings
* Flexible triple bedroom
* Two completely renovated bathrooms
* Open space kitchen
* Technologically equipped living room

Particular attention was dedicated to energy efficiency, with the installation of technological window frames, advanced insulation and low environmental impact solutions. Home automation and fast connectivity transformed the apartment into a smart and comfortable environment.

## Continuous Analysis and Improvements
Our approach did not stop at the initial renovation. We implemented a continuous monitoring system, constantly analyzing guest feedback and the property's performance.

Through a detailed analysis we:
* Optimized the workspaces
* Improved the guest experience
* Constantly adapted the furnishings and services
* Introduced elements of personalization

The results of the satisfaction questionnaires showed an approval rating above 90%, with enthusiastic comments describing the apartment not as a simple accommodation, but as an immersive experience in Florentine culture.

The continuous improvement strategy allowed us to remain competitive in an increasingly dynamic and demanding hospitality market. We transformed a real estate investment into a living and constantly evolving project, capable of generating value that is not only economic, but also experiential.

Every detail - from the paintings to the ceramics, from the furnishings to the decorative objects - tells a story, that of an extraordinary Firenze, experienced from within, like a true local.
