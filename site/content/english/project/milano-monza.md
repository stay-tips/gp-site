The metro makes every journey around the city simple and fast, and every destination becomes easily reachable, without the problem of parking

## Strategic Location and Exceptional Mobility
The proximity to the metro guarantees an immediate connection to the main areas of the city, making it possible to quickly reach both the historic center and the outlying areas. A well-connected guest house allows guests to save precious travel time, with the choice of Milano's many metro lines (M1, M2, M3, M4, M5) that cover the entire urban area extensively.

## Economic Savings on Transport
Being located near a metro station means eliminating the need for additional transport costs. ATM passes become more convenient and travel is immediate, resulting in savings both in economic terms and in the stress associated with city traffic. Transport costs can be significantly reduced, making the location a strong point for students, workers and tourists.

## Appeal for Different Types of Clients
A metropolitan guest house attracts a very broad target: university students, professionals on business trips, international tourists and temporary workers. Ease of movement and convenience of travel are a decisive factor in the choice of accommodation, potentially increasing the occupancy and revenue of the property.

## Safety and Accessibility 24/7
Milano's metro areas are generally well lit, supervised and monitored, offering a higher level of safety than more isolated areas. In addition, on certain days the metro runs frequently even during the night hours, allowing clients to move around with peace of mind and independence at any time of day or night.

## Added Value for the Stay Experience
Being close to the metro means offering your guests not just accommodation, but a complete travel experience. The ability to explore Milano easily, visiting cultural attractions, museums, restaurants and shopping areas without having to rely on taxis or car rentals is a feature of great appeal for any kind of traveler.
