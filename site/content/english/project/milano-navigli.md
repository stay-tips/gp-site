# The Story of a Gem on the Navigli

Imagine an apartment set in the beating heart of the Navigli, a small forgotten treasure just waiting to be rediscovered. This was our starting point: a property with untapped potential, awaiting a transformation that would bring it back to the center of attention of the Milanese real estate market.

![method](images/project/navigli-info-1.png)


## Benefits of the Service
When we started working on this project, the goal was clear: not just to rent out an apartment, but to tell a story. We transformed an empty space into a tale of design, functionality and charm. The results? They speak for themselves: a 35% increase in rental value, with vacancy times reduced from three long months to just two weeks.

## Business Strategy
Our strategy was like a cosmetic surgery procedure for the property. We studied the local market with the precision of a watchmaker, identifying our target: young creative professionals, lovers of design and Milanese life. Every intervention was conceived like a brushstroke: from the restyling of the interiors to the lighting, including the optimization of the spaces.

## Activity Analysis
The numbers tell only part of the story. The initial investment of 15,000 euros turned into an economic return of 22% per year. The rent jumped from 1,300 to 1,800 euros per month. But the real success? A three-year contract with an international art director who saw in the apartment not just a place to live, but an experience.

The details made the difference: the redesigned kitchen, the renovated bathroom, the lighting designed to enhance the original architectural elements. We brought life back to a space that now tells a new story, that of a property that is not just an investment, but a livable work of art.

A small gem on the Navigli, rediscovered and enhanced.
