# A Strategic Refuge a Stone's Throw from Niguarda

A few minutes from the Niguarda hospital and well connected to the center of Milano: this apartment was created for those who arrive in the city in the most delicate moments. Family members assisting with a hospital stay, patients awaiting check-ups, out-of-town healthcare staff — they all need a place that is safe, comfortable and close to where it matters to be. We transformed an outlying property with untapped potential into a point of reference for medical tourism, without giving up on attracting those who are simply looking for a strategic base from which to experience Milano.

![method](images/project/periferia-info-1.png)



# How We Transformed This Property: The 7 Phases of the Method

When the property owner contacted us, the apartment was stalled: few bookings, lukewarm reviews and a return well below its potential. Yet the location — a stone's throw from the Niguarda hospital — was a huge advantage that no one was taking advantage of. Here is what we did, step by step.

## 1. Site Visit and Suitability Assessment
We started where we always start: a thorough site visit. We do not just look at the rooms — we analyze the context, the demand in the area, the competition, the flows of people. In this case it was clear: the proximity to Niguarda generated a constant demand from family members, day-hospital patients and healthcare staff. The property had everything going for it, it just needed the right method.

## 2. Emotional Staging
An apartment is not rented out, it is made desirable. We rethought every room to convey warmth and functionality: carefully chosen furnishings, colors that convey serenity, details designed for those who arrive perhaps after a difficult day at the hospital and need to feel at home. Nothing cold or anonymous — every furnishing choice speaks of attention.

## 3. Professional Photos and Videos
Images make the difference between a distracted scroll and a booking. We produced a professional photo shoot that showcases the spaces, the natural light and the atmosphere of the apartment. The result? A listing that captures attention and converts.

## 4. Bureaucratic Procedures
Communications to the Municipality, registration with the police headquarters, tax obligations, contracts, regional regulations — all the things that give property owners a headache. We took care of it entirely, bringing the property into compliance without the owner having to fill out a single form.

## 5. Constant Cleaning and Maintenance
Every guest finds the apartment spotless, every time. We set up a professional cleaning service and scheduled maintenance that keeps the property always at its best, preserving its value over time. No detail left to chance: from the linens to the small technical interventions.

## 6. Welcome and Guest Management
Those who come to Milano for medical reasons have different needs from a classic tourist. We structured a dedicated welcome: flexible check-ins, prompt communication, availability for any need. The result is excellent reviews and guests who return or recommend us.

## 7. Dynamic Price Management
This is where revenue management comes into play. Prices are never fixed: they adapt to demand, seasonality, local events and the occupancy of nearby hospitals. This strategy brought occupancy beyond 85% per year with peaks of 95%, maximizing the return on every single night.

## The Results
The numbers speak for themselves: a 24% increase in property value and 36% higher rental profitability per year. But the most important figure is another — a relaxed property owner who no longer has to worry about anything and sees their property working at its maximum potential.
