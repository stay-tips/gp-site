If you are not satisfied with our work, we refund the entire commission.

## Why this guarantee matters

**No financial risk**
Refunding the initial commissions completely eliminates the financial risk of entrusting your property to us. You can genuinely assess our work with no strings attached.

**Our quality is guaranteed**
As professional property managers, we firmly believe in the quality of our work. We know we will have to refund you in the event of inadequate service, which is why we are committed to:
- Offering the utmost professionalism and care for your interests
- Being transparent and prompt in our communications
- Selecting the best possible tenants
- Effectively managing maintenance and issues

**Total transparency**
We put our face to what we do and take responsibility for the service. We want to build a relationship of trust with you right from the start.

**How it works**
We assess our performance together based on measurable criteria:
- The property's occupancy times
- The quality of the tenants selected
- Efficiency in managing maintenance
- The transparency of the financial reports

If we don't meet the promised standards or fail to hit the agreed targets, you can end the collaboration and be refunded.

## Conclusion

Our "satisfied or refunded" guarantee turns the traditional perspective on its head: instead of asking you for a blind act of trust, we concretely demonstrate our reliability. For you, this means greater peace of mind, financial protection and the chance to work with us with no initial risk.
