Ongoing benefits from the appreciation of a property dedicated to short-term rentals

1. Structural Appreciation
- Constant maintenance that prevents deterioration
- Regular updates to the systems
- Targeted and frequent renovations
- Continuous improvements that increase the base value
- Optimal preservation of the structure

2. Market Appreciation
- Premium positioning in the tourist segment
- Building a solid reputation
- High rating on booking platforms
- Creation of a recognisable brand
- Building loyalty among quality clientele

3. Economic Benefits
- Above-average increase in the property's value
- The ability to charge rising rates
- Accelerated ROI thanks to short-term rentals
- The ability to reinvest the higher earnings into improvements
- Faster amortisation of investments

4. Enhancement of the Area
- Contribution to the regeneration of the area
- Attraction of quality services nearby
- Improvement of the neighbourhood's reputation
- Positive effect on the prices of surrounding properties

5. Added Commercial Value
- Positive reviews accumulated over time
- Consolidated market positioning
- Network of reliable suppliers and services

6. Market Adaptability
- Ease of conversion to other uses
- Flexibility in adapting to trends
- Quick adaptation to market demands
- The possibility of diversifying the business

7. Technological Extras
- Implementation of home automation systems
- Continuous technological updates
- Energy efficiency improvements
- Constant modernisation of furnishings

These factors contribute to:
- Value growth higher than traditional properties
- Greater resilience during negative market phases
- Better bankability of the property
- Greater appeal in the event of a sale
