In order to access the property, guests pay for the entire stay in advance

Renting to tourists means not only sharing a moment of their journey and ours with them, but also enjoying a series of advantages that can be summed up as *higher rents* and *secure payments*


### Greater Profitability:
- Potential returns 20-40% higher than traditional renting
- The ability to maximise earnings during peak season
- Flexibility to adjust prices based on demand


### Financial Safeguards:
- Advance payments that guarantee the income
- Security deposits to protect against damage
- No risk of prolonged non-payment
- The ability to quickly exclude problematic guests

### Tax Benefits
- Flat-rate tax scheme (cedolare secca) for private individuals
- More deductible expenses
- The ability to amortise costs as a business activity
- Faster depreciation of furniture and furnishings
