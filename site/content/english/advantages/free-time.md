# Analysis of time savings with short-term rentals

## 1. Booking process automation
- **Digital check-in and check-out**
  - No need for physical presence, while still guaranteed for strategic guests
  - Use of smart locks and access codes that change with every entry
  - Estimated saving: 1-2 hours for every guest changeover

- **Automated booking management**
  - Automatic calendar synchronisation
  - Instant booking system
  - Estimated saving: 3-4 hours per week of manual management

## 2. Optimised communication
- **Pre-set automatic messages for**:
  - Booking confirmation
  - Periodic check-ins during the stay
  - Check-in instructions
  - Check-out reminders
  - Review requests
  - Estimated saving: 4-5 hours per week of communication

## 3. Simplified financial management
- **Automatic payments**
  - Immediate collection through the platform
  - Reporting
  - Collection of payments and taxes
  - Receipt generation
  - Estimated saving: 2-3 hours per month of accounting management

## 4. Maintenance and cleaning
- **Automated planning**
  - Automatic cleaning scheduling
  - Instant notifications to service providers
  - Real-time issue reporting system
  - Estimated saving: 4-5 hours per week of coordination

## 5. Marketing and pricing
- **Dynamic price management**
  - Automatic rate adjustment
  - Demand-based optimisation
  - Simultaneous updates across multiple platforms
  - Estimated saving: 3-4 hours per week of market analysis

## Total estimated saving
- **Weekly**: 15-20 hours
- **Monthly**: 60-80 hours
- **Annually**: 720-960 hours

## Additional considerations
1. Time savings increase proportionally with the number of properties to manage
2. Service quality improves thanks to standardised processes
3. Greater personal flexibility and a better work-life balance

## Important note
The time savings indicated assume the use of:
- Professional management software
- Smart home devices
- Reliable cleaning services
- Automated communication systems
