As owners, you can still access your property and enjoy all its comforts whenever you wish. By coordinating the booking calendar together, your home stays available to you in the moments that matter.

## The concrete benefits of keeping access to your property

**Freedom to use your home when you need it**
Imagine being able to return to your seaside home for a few days' holiday with the family, or having your mountain apartment available for the weekend. We manage the calendar together: you block off the periods you want and we take care of filling the rest with selected guests. It's like having a second home that pays for itself.

**No rigid constraints, maximum flexibility**
There's nothing inconvenient or complicated about it. You simply let us know a little in advance when you want to use the home and we organise everything; ideally in the low season. For example, if in March you decide you want to spend the mid-August holiday in your lakeside home, we simply block that week and continue to manage the bookings before and after. The home stays yours, and you keep full control of it.

**A home that is always ready and well cared for**
When you arrive, you find the home perfectly clean, tidy and fully functional. We handle all routine and extraordinary maintenance, so every time you come back it's like walking into your own little hotel. No surprises, no worries: fresh linens, a spotless bathroom, active utilities and everything in its place.

**It funds itself during the periods you don't use it**
Suppose you have a city apartment that you only use 3-4 weekends a year for events or visits. During the remaining 11 months, that same apartment generates income thanks to the short-term rentals we manage for you. In practice, guests pay the mortgage, the condominium fees and the utilities, while you keep the freedom to use it when you need to.

**Perfect for those with multiple properties**
If you own more than one home, perhaps one in the city and one by the sea or in the mountains, this service lets you optimise both without giving up your privacy. You alternate periods of personal use with rental periods, maximising both income and your own enjoyment.

**Ideal for dual-use investments**
Many of our clients buy properties with exactly this logic in mind: holiday homes in tourist destinations that they use personally for 2-3 weeks a year and that generate income for the rest of the time. It's the perfect investment: you enjoy your home during your holidays while at the same time building an asset that pays off.

## How it works in practice

The mechanism is extremely simple. At the start of the year, or whenever you prefer, you tell us the periods in which you want the home available for yourself. We block those dates in the calendar and manage all the other bookings. If your plans change, no problem: we update and reorganise accordingly.

Throughout the year you always have complete visibility over the booking calendar and the earnings. And when you arrive for your own personal stay, you find everything perfect, just the way you like it.

It's the best way to have a home that works for you when you're not using it, without ever giving up the pleasure of enjoying it in the special moments.
